Product Manager – Nimbus Metrics (Remote, EU)

Nimbus Metrics builds usage analytics for subscription businesses. Teams use
our product to see which features drive retention of their accounts and
where their onboarding loses people. We are a Series A company of about 40
people, remote across the EU.

We are hiring a Product Manager to own onboarding and activation. This is a
hands-on product role: you will run product discovery with customers, turn
what you learn into a prioritised backlog, and ship experiments on a weekly
cadence. You will read your own results in SQL, and you will work with
engineering and design to make self-serve reporting and dashboards genuinely
self-serve. You will manage stakeholders across support, sales, and
engineering, and you will own the activation funnel end to end.

What we need from you: real product discovery experience, including customer
interviews you ran yourself. Working SQL for product analytics. Hands-on
experimentation experience, designing and shipping experiments and reading
the results honestly. A track record of improving onboarding or activation
metrics somewhere real. Stakeholder management across functions.

Nice to have: experience in B2B SaaS. Familiarity with a BI tool such as
Metabase. Agile/Scrum experience. Experience rolling out product analytics
tooling to other teams.

The role is full-time, fully remote within the EU, with an annual learning
budget. Our process: intro call, hiring manager interview, a short practical
exercise about an onboarding funnel, and a final conversation with the
founders.
