Senior Embedded Firmware Engineer – Corvid Robotics (Munich, onsite)

Corvid Robotics builds robotic arms for industrial automation. Our firmware
team owns everything that runs on the arm's controllers: real-time control
loops, motor control, sensor drivers, and the safety-critical layers that
keep a two-hundred-kilogram arm predictable next to human operators.

We are looking for a senior embedded firmware engineer. You will design and
implement real-time control firmware in C and C++ on an RTOS, write and
maintain drivers for CAN bus, encoders, and motor controllers, bring up new
controller boards together with the electronics team, and own safety-critical
code paths along with the evidence our certification process requires.

Requirements: at least seven years of embedded firmware development in C and
C++. Deep RTOS experience, including scheduling, interrupt handling, and
working within tight memory constraints. Motor control experience and solid
knowledge of the CAN bus protocol. A degree in Electrical Engineering,
Computer Engineering, or an equivalent field. This role is onsite in our
Munich lab five days a week; we provide relocation support.

Nice to have: functional safety certification experience (IEC 61508) and an
interest in Rust for embedded contexts.

Our process: technical screen, an onsite day with the firmware team
including a debugging exercise on real hardware, and a final interview with
the VP of Engineering.
